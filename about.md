I am a PhD student at the [Nimbus Centre for Embedded Systems Research](http://nimbus.cit.ie) at [Cork Institute of Technology](http://www.cit.ie) (CIT). My research focuses on facilitating network control and management in Wireless Sensor Networks (WSNs). Before starting the PhD, I studied Telecommunication Engineering at [Miguel Hernández University](http://umh.es) and I did a six month internship at [emxys](http://www.emxys.com).

Among other things, I will use this blog to talk about my research and to post some Contiki or Python tutorials. If you are interested in my research, feel free to send me an email or follow me on [GitHub](https://github.com/pablocorbalan) or [Twitter](https://twitter.com/paulcorbalan). You can also connect with me on [LinkedIn](https://ie.linkedin.com/in/pablocorbalan).
